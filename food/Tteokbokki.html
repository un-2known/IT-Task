Tteokbokki 떡볶이

Tteokbokki - 떡볶이

Tteokbokki is the most famous Korean street food. You can find it in every market in Seoul. It is made of chewy rice cakes cooked in a sweet and spicy red sauce made from Gochujang, with fish cakes and boiled eggs.

It is soft, chewy, and addictive. It was originally a royal dish made with soy sauce, but now the spicy red version is the most loved.

Ingredients:
  • Rice cakes, Gochujang
  • Fish cakes, Boiled egg

Taste: Sweet, spicy, and chewy.

Best served with: Fried tempura and fish cakes.

Price: 110 LE
