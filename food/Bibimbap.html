Bibimbap - 비빔밥

Bibimbap means "mixed rice" in Korean. It is one of the most colorful and beautiful Korean dishes. A hot bowl of white rice is topped with seasoned vegetables like carrots, spinach, and mushrooms, plus beef, a fried egg, and spicy Gochujang sauce.

You mix everything together before eating. It is healthy, balanced, and full of protein and vitamins. It originated from the royal court of the Joseon Dynasty.

Ingredients:
  • Rice, Beef, Egg
  • Carrots, Spinach, Mushrooms
  • Gochujang sauce

Taste: Savory, slightly spicy, and nutty.

Fun Fact: Each color in Bibimbap represents one of the five elements in Korean philosophy.

Price: 160 LE
