Ramyun - 라면

Ramyun is Korean instant noodles, but much more delicious than regular noodles. It is a spicy soup made with chewy noodles, rich chili broth, egg, seaweed, and green onions. Sometimes cheese and kimchi are added on top.

It is the most popular comfort food for Korean students, especially on cold and rainy days. Koreans love to eat it directly from the pot.

Ingredients:
  • Noodles, Spicy broth
  • Egg, Seaweed, Green onion

Taste: Very spicy, salty, and umami.

Fun Fact: Korea is the number one country in the world for eating instant noodles.

Price: 130 LE
